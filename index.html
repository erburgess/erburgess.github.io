About

Product executive with 30 years in software, including two decades in leadership and a decade at the C-suite level. I build platforms that connect technology to human needs and market opportunity.

My career spans Disney Interactive (where I produced the Who Wants To Be A Millionaire franchise), Fortune 500 SaaS product management at Ebix, AI-powered marketing platforms at Soulmates.AI, and the founding of Credtent, a public benefit corporation building neutral licensing infrastructure for AI training data.

I write across every register: product requirements and white papers, marketing collateral and case studies, industry analysis, fiction, and personal essays, and lately, content built to earn citation from AI answer engines, not just rank in search. I am the author of two novels and the forthcoming The Collaborithmic Age, a book on AI and human creativity. My decade hosting the Boardgame Babylon podcast and consulting for tabletop publishers reflects a lifelong commitment to the creative industries.

What I bring to every role is the ability to see across disciplines, to translate research into product, strategy into narrative, and complexity into clarity. I think about content value for a living, what makes a piece of writing worth trusting, worth citing, worth paying for, on both sides of that question: as the writer, and as the founder of a company built to certify it.
